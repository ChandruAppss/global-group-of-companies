About the group

One group. Seven houses.

Global Group Of Companies is headquartered in Huai Khwang, Bangkok, and operates seven brands across Thailand and Laos.

What we do

We trade the rice, move the food, cook the meal and make the bed.

Our work follows food from start to finish. We import and export rice, distribute food from Bangkok, and serve it in our own restaurants in two capital cities.

In Vientiane, our guests can also stay with us at YT Hotel & Hostel, a short walk from the Mekong.
